Catalogue (price list)0
Your full product list for quotes & orders — you don't have to keep any of it in stock. Import it from Door Quote (or a CSV) in one go, then add lines straight onto a purchase order or invoice from the catalogue. Great when items go direct supplier → customer.
Prices from Door Quote
For the items you do keep in stock: update their unit cost & sale price from Door Quote, matched by SKU / stock code (or item name). Open Door Quote on this device first. Quantities aren't touched. (For everything else, use the Catalogue above.)
Some items won't auto-match if their SKU/name differs from Door Quote. Use Match items manually… to link them by hand — it saves the stock code so they match automatically after that.
Catalogue CSV
Import a product list from a spreadsheet — matched by SKU (or item name). It updates cost, sale price, category, supplier, location and reorder level; you can also add any new items. Download the template for the exact columns. (Export is under Export on the stock screen.)
Backup
Save a copy of your whole inventory to a file on this device — a safety net that doesn't depend on GitHub. Keep it somewhere safe; restore anytime.
